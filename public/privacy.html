Privacy Policy

This describes what the demo actually stores and how it is handled.

What we store

Your name, email, phone number, and a secure hash of your password. Your reports, including type, note, location, and any photo. Any identity images you upload during sign up. A log of sensitive actions for security.

How passwords are handled

Passwords are never stored in plain text. They are hashed with bcrypt. Staff cannot see your password.

Who can see your data

Operators and admins can see reports and the reporter contact details so they can respond. Identity images are shown only to reviewers and are served through access controlled links, never in a public folder.

Location

Location is shared only when you choose to add it to a report. You can drag the marker before sending.

Security

The app uses server side sessions, protection against cross site request forgery, rate limits on sign in, and security headers. For a real deployment the site must run over HTTPS and identity images should move to encrypted storage.

To confirm before real use

Data retention period, the legal basis for processing, and how to request deletion of your data must be set by the municipality.
