Demonstration build. Please read what this app does and does not do.

What this app is

This app lets residents of the town report incidents and receive alerts from the municipality. It is a demonstration and is not connected to real emergency services. In an emergency, call the numbers listed in the app.

Accounts

You sign up with your email and a password. You confirm your email through a link we send you. An operator then reviews your account before you can submit reports. Roles above resident are granted only by a town admin.

Reports

When you submit a report you may share your location and a photo. Operators and admins can see your report, your name, and your phone number so they can respond.

Acceptable use

Do not submit false reports. Do not upload content that is unlawful or that belongs to someone else. Misuse can lead to account rejection.

To confirm before real use

The following are placeholders in this build and must be set by the municipality before real use: legal entity and contact, data retention period, the exact review process for identity documents, and the emergency numbers shown.
